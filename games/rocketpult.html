Rocketpult

Wind up the catapult, launch your rocket, and steer it mid-air to land on the platforms below. Distance and clean landings both score points — fuel runs out fast, so make it count.

Play Rocketpult

Controls

  • ↑ thrust
  • ← → rotate mid-air
  • Space launch / deploy landing legs
  • Z slow time
  • Backspace hold to reset

Tips

Tap thrust in short bursts rather than holding it — you'll get more distance per unit of fuel. Near a platform, flip the rocket so the engine faces down and brake with short thrusts before deploying the legs.
