Snow Rider 3D

Steer a sled down an endless snowy slope, dodge trees, rocks and snowmen, and grab presents along the way for a better score. Small, early corrections beat sharp last-second swerves.

Snow Rider 3D is a quick standalone run rather than a saved campaign, so there's no ongoing progress to remember between visits — just jump in and go for distance.

Controls

  • ← → steer left / right
  • ↑ or Space jump over gaps and ramps

Tips

Stay near the middle of the track so you have room to react either way. Make small steering adjustments early rather than sharp swerves at the last second, and the run speeds up the farther you go, so plan a little ahead of what's on screen.
