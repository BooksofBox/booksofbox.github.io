Papa's Freezeria

Layer the ice cream, blend in the mix-ins, and top it exactly how each customer ordered it before the line backs up. Precision on the blender step is what separates a good tip from a great one.

Papa's Freezeria has its own save slots built in — pick one the first time you play, and your progress will be right there next time, as long as you keep playing from this page in the same browser.

Controls

  • Mouse — click stations, ingredients and the order ticket

How it works

Pour the base, blend in candies and syrups for the right amount of time, then add toppings and a cherry to finish. Watch the blend timer closely — over- or under-mixing costs tip points.

Papa's Freezeria is made by Flipline Studios. This page plays the game through Miniplay's official embeddable player — no game files are hosted on this site.
