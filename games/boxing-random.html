Boxing Random

One-button ragdoll boxing. Land punches on your opponent's head to score, watch for the rocket-punch rounds that can knock you out from any position, and be the first to five points.

Boxing Random is a quick standalone match rather than a saved campaign, so there's no ongoing progress to remember between visits — just jump in and play.

Controls

  • W punch — Player 1
  • ↑ punch — Player 2

How it works

Face the CPU solo or share the keyboard for 2-player. The arena, outfits and even the boxers' arm length can change each round, so stay light on your feet and watch for the rare rocket-punch round.
