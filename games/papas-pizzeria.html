Papa's Pizzeria

Run the counter solo: take each order, build the pizza to spec, time the oven right, and slice it up before the next customer loses patience. Tips add up fast once your stations stop colliding.

Papa's Pizzeria has its own save slots built in — pick one the first time you play, and your progress, unlocks, and regulars will be right there next time, as long as you keep playing from this page in the same browser.

Controls

  • Mouse — click stations, ingredients and the order ticket

How it works

Grab the order, build the pizza on the topping station, bake it for the right time, then cut and box it. Regulars remember their usual order — check the customer book when it unlocks.

Papa's Pizzeria is made by Flipline Studios. This page plays the game through Miniplay's official embeddable player — no game files are hosted on this site.
