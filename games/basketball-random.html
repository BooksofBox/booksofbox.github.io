Basket Random

First team to five baskets wins — but the court, the ball, and your player's proportions can change after almost every score. Play solo against the CPU or share the keyboard for two-player.

Basket Random is a quick standalone match rather than a saved campaign, so there's no ongoing progress to remember between visits — just jump in and play.

Controls

  • W jump & shoot — Player 1
  • ↑ jump & shoot — Player 2

How it works

Choose 1 Player to face the CPU, or 2 Players for local head-to-head. Timing matters more than aim — jump into the ball and let the ragdoll physics do the rest.

Basket Random is made by RHM Interactive. This page plays the game directly from G2K, which is RHM Interactive's own site — no game files are hosted here.
